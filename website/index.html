02 / PERSONAL BY NATURE

Your entertainment.
Your boundaries.

You should decide what stays on your device, what connects, and what learns from you. That is the design principle behind AURA.

Read the full strategy ↗
01

Start local

Local-first AURA accounts are the goal. Connected services may still require their own accounts, subscriptions and internet access.

02

Connect on your terms

AuraConnect is planned as optional encrypted sync, with manual or automatic sharing controls. Encryption architecture and recovery need validation before release.

03

A guardian, with limits

Aura Guardian is planned to monitor supported security signals and integrity changes within platform permissions. It cannot promise to detect every attack.

04

Personalization by invitation

Optional on-device learning is the goal. Third-party APIs can still receive requests, IP addresses and service-related data when you choose to connect them.

03 / BUILDING IN THE OPEN

Milestones, not promises

Good things take intention.

01 · NOW

Lay the foundation

Define the experience, document privacy boundaries and develop the website. Product readiness is not yet verified here.

02 · NEXT

Learn with a small beta

Invite a small testing group once install, navigation and privacy controls pass release checks. No beta registration is open yet.

03 · LATER

Open the doors

Expand access after stability, accessibility and security reviews. Publish supported devices, integrations and real release notes.
