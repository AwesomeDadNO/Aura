PERSONAL BY NATURE

Your entertainment.
Your boundaries.

Local control is our starting point. Connections, learning and sharing should be choices you understand.

01

Start with yourself

Local-first AURA accounts are the goal. Connected providers may require separate accounts and subscriptions.

02

Choose what connects

AuraConnect is planned as optional encrypted sync, with manual or automatic sharing. Architecture and encryption still need validation.

03

Let Aura in. Or don't.

Guardian security signals and optional on-device personalization are planned. Platform permissions limit protection; third-party APIs can receive service requests and IP addresses.

A WORK IN PROGRESS

Product direction, privacy boundaries and this experimental website. App readiness is not verified in this repository.

02 / NEXT
